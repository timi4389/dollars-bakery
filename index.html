About Us

The Baker Behind Dollars Bakery

Behind Dollars Bakery is a baker with six years of hands-on experience creating cakes for birthdays, weddings, house parties and every celebration in between. What began as a genuine love for baking has grown into a busy bakery at T Poly, Kpalsi, serving customers across the area.

Every cake is baked fresh to order and finished by hand — from simple birthday cakes to elegant two-tier wedding cakes, celebration cakes, gift boxes and money bouquets. We pay close attention to taste, texture and presentation, because a cake should look as good as it tastes.

When you choose Dollars Bakery, you are working with someone who genuinely cares about your occasion. Whether it is a small family birthday or a large celebration, we will help you get every detail right.

  • Six years of hands-on baking experience
  • Cakes, pastries and gift boxes made fresh to order
  • Friendly, personal service from order to delivery
Based at T Poly, Kpalsi, Ghana
